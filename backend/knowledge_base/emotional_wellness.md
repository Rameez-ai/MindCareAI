# Emotional Wellness and Self-Regulation

Emotional wellness is the ability to navigate your feelings, adapt to challenges, and bounce back from setbacks. It is not about feeling happy all the time, but about developing a healthy relationship with all of your emotions.

## 1. Emotional Awareness (Naming Emotions)
- **Identify and Label**: Use the 'Feeling Wheel' or similar vocabulary. Instead of saying "I feel bad," be specific: "I feel rejected," "I feel overwhelmed," or "I feel disappointed."
- **Observe without Judgment**: Notice where you feel the emotion in your body (e.g., tight chest, heavy stomach) and let it exist without trying to force it away.

## 2. Healthy Coping Mechanisms
- **Expressive Writing**: Journal for 10-15 minutes about your thoughts and feelings without worrying about grammar or structure. This helps clarify and externalize emotions.
- **Physical Activity**: Exercise releases endorphins, which act as natural mood lifters. Even a brisk 10-minute walk can help break a negative thought cycle.
- **Connection**: Share your feelings with a trusted friend, family member, or mental health professional. You don't have to navigate hard feelings in isolation.
