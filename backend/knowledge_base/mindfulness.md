# Mindfulness and Meditation Guide

Mindfulness is the practice of being fully present in the current moment, aware of where we are and what we are doing, and not overly reactive or overwhelmed by what is going on around us.

## 1. Core Mindfulness Practices
- **Mindful Breathing**: Spend 5 minutes focusing solely on the sensation of your breath entering and leaving your nostrils, or the rising and falling of your chest. If your mind wanders, gently guide it back without judgment.
- **Body Scan Meditation**: Lie down or sit comfortably. Move your attention slowly from your toes up to your head, noting any sensations (tingling, warmth, tightness) without trying to change them.
- **Five Senses Grounding**: Focus on 5 things you can see, 4 things you can touch, 3 things you can hear, 2 things you can smell, and 1 thing you can taste.

## 2. Dealing with Negative Thoughts Mindfully
- **Thought Labeling**: Instead of getting caught up in a thought, label it: "I am having a thought that I am going to fail." This creates distance between you and the thought.
- **Leaves on a Stream Exercise**: Imagine sitting by a stream, with leaves floating down. Place each thought, feeling, or sensation onto a leaf and watch it float away.
- **Self-Compassion**: Talk to yourself with the same kindness you would show a close friend when they are struggling.
