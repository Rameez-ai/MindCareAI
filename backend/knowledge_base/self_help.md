# Self-Help Guidance

Self-help practices empower you to take an active role in managing your mental well-being. Consistently implementing small, positive changes can lead to significant improvements in your overall mood.

## 1. Setting Micro-Goals
Large goals can feel overwhelming, leading to procrastination and anxiety.
- **Break it Down**: Divide a large goal into the smallest possible actionable steps.
- **Celebrate Progress**: Acknowledge and appreciate yourself for completing each step.
- **Consistency over Intensity**: Doing 5 minutes of study or exercise every day is more effective than doing 2 hours once a week.

## 2. Healthy Daily Routine
A structured daily routine provides stability and predictability, reducing cognitive fatigue.
- **Regular Meals**: Eat balanced meals at consistent times to maintain stable blood sugar levels.
- **Hydration**: Drink plenty of water throughout the day. Dehydration can mimic anxiety symptoms.
- **Nature and Light**: Spend 10-15 minutes outdoors, especially in the morning, to help regulate your circadian rhythm.
