# Sleep Hygiene and Improvement

Sleep is fundamental to mental health. Chronic insomnia can exacerbate anxiety, depression, and stress. Building healthy sleep habits helps restore cognitive functioning and emotional balance.

## 1. Core Principles of Sleep Hygiene
- **Consistent Schedule**: Go to bed and wake up at the same time every day, even on weekends.
- **Cool, Dark Room**: Ensure your bedroom is quiet, dark, and kept at a cool temperature (around 65°F / 18°C).
- **The Bed is for Sleep Only**: Avoid working, watching TV, or eating in bed. This associates the bed with alertness.
- **Limit Blue Light**: Turn off screens (phones, computers) at least 60 minutes before bedtime.

## 2. Dealing with Late Night Worry (Insomnia)
- **The 20-Minute Rule**: If you cannot fall asleep after 20 minutes, get out of bed. Go to a dimly lit room and do a quiet, relaxing activity (like reading or breathing exercises) until you feel sleepy.
- **Worry Journal**: If thoughts keep you awake, write them down in a notebook to "unload" your brain before trying to sleep.
- **Brain Dump**: Write a to-do list for tomorrow to let your brain know it is safe to rest.
