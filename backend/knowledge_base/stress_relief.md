# Stress Relief and Burnout Prevention

Stress is an inevitable part of life, but chronic stress can lead to physical and emotional exhaustion (burnout). Managing stress requires building healthy habits, setting boundaries, and using relaxation techniques.

## 1. Setting Healthy Boundaries
Stress is often caused by taking on too much responsibility.
- **The Power of 'No'**: Say no to non-essential commitments that drain your energy.
- **Digital Detox**: Set clear hours for checking work emails and social media. Avoid screens at least 1 hour before bed.
- **Assertive Communication**: Express your needs and limits clearly and respectfully.

## 2. Physiological Sigh
A quick, scientifically-backed way to instantly reduce stress:
1. Take a deep inhalation through the nose.
2. At the very top, take a second, sharp sniff to fully inflate the lungs.
3. Exhale slowly and completely through the mouth.
4. Repeat 2 to 3 times for a rapid reduction in physiological arousal.

## 3. Daily Stress Reframing
- Shift from a threat mindset ("This is going to ruin me") to a challenge mindset ("This is difficult, but I will learn from it").
- Focus on what is within your control, and let go of what is outside your control.
